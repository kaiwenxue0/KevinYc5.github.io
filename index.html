Ph.D. student · Generative modeling

Kaiwen Xue 薛凯文

Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence
Renmin University of China

I am a Ph.D. student at Renmin University of China, advised by Prof. Chongxuan Li. I received my B.E. from the School of Information and Software Engineering at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China in 2023.

My research focuses on deep generative models, especially diffusion models and Bayesian flow networks. I am interested in understanding their underlying dynamics and developing efficient algorithms for continuous and discrete data.
